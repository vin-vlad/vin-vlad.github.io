2. Service Description

Nomopics is an iOS photo cleaner. It lets you swipe through photos and videos in your Photo Library, keep what you want, queue the rest, and confirm before anything is deleted.

  • Photo and video processing happens locally on your device
  • The Service Provider does not upload, host, or back up your Photo Library
  • Nothing is deleted until you confirm; iOS then typically moves items to Recently Deleted

3. Photo Library and Deletions

The Application requires Photo Library access to show and delete media. You are responsible for the items you queue and confirm for deletion.

Confirmed deletions use Apple’s Photos framework. Recoverability after deletion depends on iOS (including the Recently Deleted album and its retention period). The Service Provider cannot restore media that you confirmed for deletion.

If you close the Application before confirming, the in-memory delete queue is cleared and those items are not deleted.
