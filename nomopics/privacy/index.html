Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Nomopics (“we,” “our,” or “us”), developed by Vladyslav Palamarchuk, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

Nomopics is an iOS photo cleaner. You swipe through photos and videos in your Photo Library, keep what you want, queue the rest, and confirm before anything is deleted. Photo and video processing happens locally on your device. We do not upload your photos, videos, or Photo Library contents to our servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

Nomopics does not collect, store, or transmit to us:

  • Photo or video files, thumbnails, or image content
  • The contents of your Photo Library as a backup or cloud copy
  • Your name, email address, or an account (no account is required)
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, messages, or other unrelated device content

Core cleaning features — browsing folders, swiping, queuing, and deleting — operate on your device using Apple’s Photos framework (PhotoKit).

Photo Library Access

Nomopics requests read and write access to your Photo Library so the app can:

  • Display your photos and videos in folders (Recents, months, albums, and similar collections)
  • Let you swipe to keep an item or add it to a delete queue
  • Delete items you explicitly confirm, using Apple’s standard deletion flow

Confirmed deletions are handled by iOS. Items typically move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where Apple’s usual recovery period applies. We do not receive a copy of the media you delete.

On your device, Nomopics may store Photo Library identifiers (not the images themselves) so the app can remember which folders you have already reviewed. The in-memory delete queue is cleared if the app is closed before you confirm deletion.

You can change Photo Library access at any time in iOS Settings. Limited access is supported; you can expand the selection from Nomopics Settings.

Advertising

Unless you have purchased Remove Ads, Nomopics shows interstitial ads (currently after every 25 photos you view) through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC.

If you allow App Tracking on iOS, ads may be personalized. If you decline or later turn tracking off, you may still see ads, but they are less likely to be based on your advertising identifier.

Google may process limited technical and advertising data in accordance with Google’s policies, which can include:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA), if you have allowed tracking
  • IP address and approximate region
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and similar ad-performance events

This data is used to deliver, measure, and (where permitted) personalize ads. It is processed by Google, not stored by us as a photo or library dataset.

You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. You can also remove ads on this device with a one-time in-app purchase.

Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services
  • AdMob privacy and data use

In-App Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payment processing. You can restore a previous purchase from Nomopics Settings.

Local App Data

Nomopics stores a small amount of data only on your device, such as:

  • Language preference
  • Whether the tracking prompt has already been shown
  • Folder review progress (photo identifiers, not image files)
  • The Remove Ads entitlement, managed by Apple

This local data is removed if you delete the app, except for App Store purchase records retained by Apple.

Third-Party Service Providers

Nomopics uses:

  • Google LLC — Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising
  • Apple Inc. — Photo Library access, App Store distribution, and in-app purchases

These providers process limited data as needed to provide their services, under their own privacy policies. We do not operate a Nomopics backend that receives your photos.

Legal Basis for Processing (EEA / UK Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or similar jurisdictions:

  • Photo Library access is based on your permission and is necessary to provide the app’s cleaning features.
  • Personalized advertising is based on your consent (App Tracking).
  • Non-personalized advertising may be based on our legitimate interest in offering a free, ad-supported app.
  • In-app purchases are processed to perform the contract you enter when you buy Remove Ads.

You may withdraw tracking consent at any time in iOS Settings, and you may revoke Photo Library access in iOS Settings. Withdrawing Photo Library permission will prevent the app from showing or deleting your media.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We do not share your photos, videos, or Photo Library contents with third parties.

Advertising data may be processed by Google solely to provide ads. Purchase data is processed by Apple to complete and restore purchases.

Data Retention

Photos and videos remain in your Photo Library on your device (and, after a confirmed delete, in Recently Deleted according to iOS). We do not retain copies.

Local preferences last until you change them or delete the app. Advertising data is retained according to Google’s policies. Purchase records are retained according to Apple’s policies.

Children

Nomopics is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of applicable data
  • Withdraw consent for tracking or Photo Library access
  • Object to certain processing activities

Because most Nomopics data never leaves your device, the most direct controls are iOS Settings (Photos and Tracking) and the Remove Ads purchase. To exercise other rights, contact us at: support@nomoads.com
